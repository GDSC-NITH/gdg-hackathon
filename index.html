FAQ & Rules

FAQ
Is there a registration fee?

No. Labyrinth is completely free. Meals, snacks and swag during the event are covered.

What is the team size?

Teams of 2 to 4 members. Each team names one team leader when registering.

Who can participate?

Any college student with a valid student ID. Students from all branches and years are welcome, including first-time hackers.

I don’t have a team. Can I still join?

Yes. Register on your own and we will help you find teammates before hacking starts.

Can we work on an existing project?

No. All code and designs must be made during the 36 hours. Open-source libraries, public APIs and starter templates are allowed.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger, student ID, any hardware you plan to use, and a water bottle. Bring a blanket if you plan to rest overnight.

Will food and accommodation be provided?

Meals and snacks are provided throughout the hackathon. The venue stays open overnight for hacking.

How are projects judged?

On innovation, technical complexity, design and usability, and impact. Teams demo to the judging panel at the end.

The nearest airport is Gaggal (Kangra), about 90 km away. Chandigarh airport is the nearest major airport.

Check in at the New Lecture Hall on Jan 17 from 09:00. Volunteers will guide you from the main gate.
